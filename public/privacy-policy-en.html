JOMON PIX

Privacy Policy

Operator: Jomon
Effective and last updated: November 15, 2026

Jomon (the “Operator”) establishes this policy regarding the handling of information in JOMON PIX, a photo-editing application for iOS and the web (the “App”).

1. Photos and editing data

Photos imported into the App, edited images, stickers, text, drawings, layout information and other project data are generally processed and stored on the user's device or in the browser. Work in progress is automatically saved in IndexedDB on the device. If project data from an older version remains in localStorage, it may be retained as a recovery copy.

The current App does not provide a feature to upload and store users' photos or projects on the Operator's servers or to publish them to other users. The Operator does not provide an administration interface for viewing projects stored on users' devices.

Facial landmarks used for face detection, face decoration and beauty adjustments are also designed to be processed on the device. The App has no mechanism for transmitting face photos or landmarks to the Operator's servers.

2. Access to photos on your device

The App accesses images as needed when a user chooses a photo or takes another relevant action. It does not automatically collect photos the user has not selected. Depending on the device, photo access permissions may be changed in device settings.

3. External connections and third-party services

Photo editing generally takes place on the device. However, the following external connections may occur:

  • Google Fonts: Fonts used for text display may be downloaded from Google's font delivery service.
  • Background removal: When this feature is used, the IMG.LY background-removal library may retrieve AI model files and related resources from external distributors. In the current implementation, the background-removal computation itself runs on the device; photos are not sent to an image-processing server operated by the Operator.
  • Web version: Connections are made to web hosting services, including GitHub Pages, to deliver the site.

For external connections, the receiving services may obtain technical information such as IP addresses, browser or device information, and access times, and handle it according to their own policies. These connections are not intended to publish users' photos.

The current App has no advertising distributed by the Operator, advertising-related tracking, account registration requirement, or feature that sends photos to a proprietary analytics service.

4. Saving, exporting and sharing

When a user chooses to save or share an image, it is prepared as a temporary file on the device and passed to the selected destination through the iOS sharing interface or the browser's download features. Information sent to other apps or cloud services is subject to the receiving service's practices.

A JSON project backup includes the actual photos and editing content. Take care before sending it to others or publishing it. Backups or synchronization through Photos, Files, iCloud or similar services depend on the device and service settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Local editing data may remain until the user edits or replaces a project, deletes browser site data, or removes the App's data. Older localStorage records and temporary sharing files may persist even when a project is replaced.

Because user projects are not stored on the Operator's servers, the Operator cannot remotely delete photos stored on a user's device. Users may clear the App's data or browser site data if needed. Doing so may make edited projects unrecoverable. Saved PNG or JSON files and files already shared elsewhere must be deleted separately.

If a user voluntarily sends an inquiry by email, information such as their email address, name and message will be used to respond and handled for as long as needed for that purpose. Please contact us below regarding deletion requests.

6. Security

The Operator aims to avoid unnecessary transmission of users' photos to external services. However, risks such as device loss, unauthorized operations, OS or browser vulnerabilities, and user-initiated sharing cannot be completely eliminated.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the App's features or external services change. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page. The updated text and revision date will be displayed here.
